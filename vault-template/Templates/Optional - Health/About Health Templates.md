> [!warning] Optional and structure only
> These four templates (appointment, medication, provider, record) give you property names and headings, nothing more. They contain no health content and are not medical advice. Record only what a visit, letter or prescription says, keep dates and values exactly as the source states them, and leave unknown properties blank.

Health notes are sensitive. Before you use these, add a `Health/` folder, give it a nested `CLAUDE.md` that tells Claude not to interpret, summarize for others, or send the contents anywhere (the starter [[CLAUDE]] has a placeholder section), and consider keeping the folder out of git.

Delete this folder if you do not want it.
